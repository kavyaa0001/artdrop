Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information ArtDrop Wallpapers collects, how it's used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Order information: name, email, and payment details you provide at checkout (payment card data is handled directly by our payment processor — we never store full card numbers).
  • Contact information: anything you send us by email, such as support requests.
  • Usage data: basic analytics and browser information (device type, pages visited) collected automatically to understand how the site is used and to prevent abuse.

2. How we use this information

  • To process your order and deliver your Product;
  • To respond to support requests;
  • To improve the site and prevent fraud or unauthorized access to paid content;
  • To send order-related communications (we do not sell your data to third parties).

3. Cookies and tracking pixels

We use essential cookies and lightweight tracking pixels (for example, to measure ad performance and checkout completion) so we can understand which pages are working and keep the store running smoothly. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

4. Third-party services

We rely on trusted third parties to run the store, including a payment processor for checkout and standard hosting/analytics providers. These providers only receive the information needed to perform their function.

5. Data retention

We keep order records for as long as needed for accounting, legal, and support purposes.

6. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing us.
